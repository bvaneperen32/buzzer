Confirmed — `Cannot open MySQL80 service on computer '.'` is the access-denied message. It needs an elevated shell.

**Open PowerShell as Administrator** (Win+X → *Terminal (Admin)*), then:

```powershell
Stop-Service MySQL80
Set-Service MySQL80 -StartupType Manual
```

The second line matters: `StartType` is currently `Automatic`, so without it MySQL80 comes back on your next reboot and you'll hit this same wall again with no idea why.

Or, from your normal terminal, this one-liner triggers the UAC prompt and does both in a new window:

```powershell
Start-Process powershell -Verb RunAs -ArgumentList '-NoExit','-Command','Stop-Service MySQL80; Set-Service MySQL80 -StartupType Manual; Get-Service MySQL80'
```

Expect `Status: Stopped`, `StartType: Manual`.

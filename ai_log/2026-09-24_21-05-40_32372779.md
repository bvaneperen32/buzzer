## What Buzzer is

Buzzer is a real-time quiz game for a room, similar to Jackbox or Kahoot. It has three kinds of users:

- **Host:** puts the game on a projector: the question, a QR code for joining, and the live answer counts. Hosts also write games and questions and manage class rosters.
- **Players:** join on their phones and tap answers. The phone shows only the answer buttons, not the question.
- **Admins:** manage users and courses, and decide who can host or play which games.
